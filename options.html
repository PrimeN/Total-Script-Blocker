What gets shared?

Lookups send the selected domain to WOT, without its URL path or query string. Keys stay in trusted extension storage and are excluded from exports. Automatic checks are off by default. Results are cached for 30 minutes; WOT account quotas apply. An unavailable or low-confidence rating is not proof of safety.

Chrome Sync

Sync saved trusted and blocked domains, protection controls and toolbar preferences. Enable extension syncing in Chrome on each device and use the same extension ID. Chrome queues changes while offline.

Temporary grants, pause state, discovered domains and WOT credentials stay on this device. Enable sync separately on each device. Concurrent edits may require choosing a copy.

Choosing a copy replaces the other settings. A local backup is kept before incoming settings are applied. Restore turns off sync on this device. Turning sync off keeps the copy in Chrome Sync.

Coverage & recovery

Protection controls HTTP/HTTPS network requests with declarative rules and appended Content Security Policy. It cannot undo code that already ran.

Where protection is limited

Website service-worker/CacheStorage-generated documents may bypass response-header rules. File/FTP URLs, browser/extension pages, non-network documents and incognito are outside this version's coverage. Website CSP may remain stricter than your extension rules.

How to read domain discovery

Page declarations, observed requests and browser/CSP denials are different evidence. A completed response does not prove execution, and a browser denial may come from another extension or the website. Toolbar counts represent distinct domains restricted by the domain policy, not blocked requests.

Parent domains, inheritance and session grants

Blocking a child domain preserves its parent's permanent trust and adds a denial for that child. Inherit removes only the exact rule. Granting a domain can replace overlapping rules. Temporary grants override denials; changing an overlapping temporary parent grant may affect siblings.

Reloading ends temporary grants and clears the pause state.
